Why should you join a clan

You should join a clan because it is an easy way to speed up your porgression by giving you free cards every week and if you donate card you get xp and cards.

Clan wars

Clan wars happen every week and last for half of the week. There are two types of clan wars there is race and king of the hill. In the race game mode each day which ever clan has the most medels will move the furthist and the firt clan to reach the end wins. For the king of the hill game mode which ever clan with the most meadles by the end of the week wins.
