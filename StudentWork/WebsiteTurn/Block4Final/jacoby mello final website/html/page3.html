Card types

Win conditions

Win conditions are cards that reliably direct samage to enemy tower.

spells

Spells are cards you can place anywhere. There are two types of spells, there are Direct Damage spells such as fireball or arrows, which deal damage instantly and support spells, which deal damage over time, stun or move troops. For example poison, frieeze, of snowball

Troops

Troops are most of the card you will use. When they are placed they will spawn the corresponding character. There are many types of troops including: flying, crowd control, swarm, tank, building, and support.
