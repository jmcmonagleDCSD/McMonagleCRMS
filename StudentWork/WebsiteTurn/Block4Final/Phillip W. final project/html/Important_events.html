Top 1 History

Top 1's

Here's a list of (in my opinion) the most notable top 1 hardest levels.

  • Bloodbath - The oldest level mentioned here, verified in 2017 by a previous top player named Riot. Fun fact: this is the level I am currently trying to beat :)
  • Sonic wave - Another old level which has decoration inspired by the level Nine Circles; the level taht influenced an entire genre of levels, and it's still around nowadays. Originally "verified" by Cyclic (he cheated the level) and then later actually verified by Sunix.
  • Bloodlust - The sequel level to Bloodbath verified by Knobbelboy in 2018. Being almost a minute longer and having MUCH harder gameplay, this level would stay in the top 150 hardest levels in the game up until recently in 2025.
  • Zodiac - This would be the level to dethrone Bloodlust as the top one in April 2019, and is known for being a very fun top level. Verified by Technical (A content creator/top player) in around 20,000 attempts.
  • Tartarus - The most famous old top 1, verified by Dolphy on January 6, 2020. This level would stay as the top 1 for almost 1 and a half YEARS.
  • Slaughterhouse - Dethroned Tartarus as the top 1 on October 24, 2021. Originally hack verified by Spaceuk, the most infamous cheater in Geometry dash history, It is commonly called the level when players grew... you know what. Actually verified by Doggie in around 79,000 attempts.
  • Flamewall - Finally in the present, this level recently got verified by the player Cuatrocientos in around 200,000 attempts. He had been grnding and streaming the level for longer than a year (I believe in 410 streams; streaming daily). Probably one of the greatest top 1's to ever be made, lasting 7 minutes long and boasting my favorite song for a top 1 ever - Flamewall by Camellia.
