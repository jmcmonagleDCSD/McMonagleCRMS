Professional Players and their Achievments

Zoink

Zoink is often credited to be the greatest player in the game currently. He is known for beating the ENTIRE top 75 hardest verified levels in the game in April of 2023. He has also verified not one, not two, not three, but four top ones. No other player has ever done that, and he did it consecutively! This man has also gotten a previously thought impossible level into two runs --- (0-67 and 65-100). It is absolutely mindblowing to look at everything he has done in this game, I seriously recommend checking it out.

Wpopoff

Wpopoff is a 15 year old kid who is either known for being the best player currently or the second best. He is known for beating two previous top 1's at the age of 13, which is absolutely insane to think about. Some other notable completions include; beating slaughterhouse (a former top 1, now number 18 on the demonlist) 3 times in a row, getting 87% of the way through the current top 1, Thinking Space 2, in only 15,000 attempts which is completely unheard of. Especially considering it took Zoink nearly 6 months to verify the level.

Doggie

Doggie is a top player who is currently verifying what would be the hardest level in the game by far. Called "Grief" this level is nearly 3 minutes long and has 237 60fps frame perfects. Do you realize how hard it is to even do one 60 fps frame perfect click? There is also technically much harder clicks, upwards of 240 fps frame perfects. He has been working on this level for over a year now and as of may 2025 has 268,269 attempts on Grief, and still has 80% on the level.

Zeronium

Zeronium is a top 86 player who has beaten over 800 extreme demons (the hardest difficulty of level, only ~1,200 in the game) as of July 18th 2025. He (?) is my personal favorite top player as he makes very entertaining content on his youtube channel and twitch channel. The hardest level he has beaten is Deimos (currently number 21 on the demonlist) and has verified a top 30 level named Gaggatrondra as well.
