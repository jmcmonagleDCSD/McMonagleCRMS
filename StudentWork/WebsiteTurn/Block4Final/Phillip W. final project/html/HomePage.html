Geometry Dash: The Hardest Game Ever Made

Img by huyvo2001 on DeviantArt

Geometry Dash is a side scroller game known for being one of, if not the hardest game to play professionally in. Some of the top hardest levels have hundreds of frame perfects and nearly perfect precision needed to beat them. Only a few people are up to the challenge to even try, are you?

Why is it the hardest?

Here's the thing, there isn't really a way to measure difficulty in games, especially if they use completely different skillsets. So what I'm going to do to convince you is bring up the absolutely insane things the top levels force you to do.

  • Slaughterhouse - If you want to see the level yourself, go to the "Top Players" webpage and look for it. I'd recommend watching the level to really put into scale how difficult it is, but here's some things anyways. It has a total of 65 frame perfects (on 60fps), with gameplay that gets up to 10 clicks per second, and all of those clicks have to be mindnumbingly precise.
  • Amethyst - A current top 3 hardest level that got verified over 4 months ago. It has a total of 13 120 fps frame perfects and 143 60 fps frame perfects. Yes, you are reading that right, there is 13 moments in this level where you are expected to hit a window that is as long as 1/120th of a second, CONSISTENLY.
I know that a lot of people will argue with this and say games like Rocket League are harder. And yes, I realize that simply playing the game as a normal person who doesn't want to push the game's limits isn't really all that hard. But I do believe that beating a top 50 level is one of the hardest things to do in gaming (at least the hardest single thing, not including stuff like "beating Elden Ring without dying 500 times in a row"). If you want to disagree, believe what you want, but I seriously think playing Geometry Dash at the top level is the hardest thing in gaming.
