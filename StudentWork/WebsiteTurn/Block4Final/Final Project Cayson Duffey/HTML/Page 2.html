What is Blox Fruits



Blox Fruits is an RPG and PVE game on the game platform roblox. It is based off of the hit action anime series One Piece. In total there is three seas that are in the game witch are like new maps/places and you go to each one as you progress through the game. The levels to get to each sea are: First Sea - level 1-700 - Second Sea - level 700-1500 - Third Sea - level 1500 ECT.
