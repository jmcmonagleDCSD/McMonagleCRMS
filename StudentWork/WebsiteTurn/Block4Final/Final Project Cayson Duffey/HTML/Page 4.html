Fruits



What is a Blox Fruit. A blox fruit is a item in the game that once you eat it you get powers that cna help you fight the NPCS. You are able to buy permant fruits but you can get free ones in game with money you get from fighting NPCS and leveling up. There are a total of 3 types of fruits in the game Beast-Type, Elemental-Type, and Natural-Type. The best fruits in the game are Kitsune, Dragon, Leopard, and Dough due to their pvp skills. Some other good ones are Bhudda, Portal, Venom, Shadow, Soul, Rumble, and the awakened versions of Magma and Ice.
