The NCAA

The National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) is an organization that administers intercollegiate athletics in the United States. It formulates rules of play and eligibility criteria, supervises athletic contests, and conducts national championships in various sports, including ice hockey. The NCAA compiles statistics and publishes rule books for a dozen college sports. Member colleges can select the division they belong to, with each division holding national championship competitions.

The AHL

The American Hockey League (AHL) is the primary developmental league for the National Hockey League (NHL). It provides a proving ground for up-and-coming prospects and serves as the highest tier of professional hockey below the NHL in North America.

The NHA

The National Hockey Association (NHA) was a professional ice hockey league in Canada from 1909 to 1917, and it served as the precursor to the modern National Hockey League (NHL).
