Trophies

The Stanley Cup

The Stanley Cup is the championship trophy awarded annually to the winner of the National Hockey League (NHL) playoffs. It is the oldest professional sports trophy in North America, with a history that dates back to 1892.

The Memorial Cup

The Memorial Cup is the championship trophy awarded annually to the champion of the Canadian Hockey League (CHL), Canada's top major junior ice hockey league. It is distinct from the NHL's Stanley Cup, which is awarded to the champions of professional hockey.

The IIHF Championship Trophy

The International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF) awards several trophies, most notably the IIHF World Championship Trophy, to the winner of its annual men's tournament. The IIHF also presents trophies for other tournaments and awards to outstanding individuals.

The Calder Cup

The Calder Cup is the AHL's championship trophy, awarded annually to the playoff champion. Named after Frank Calder, the first president of the National Hockey League (NHL), the trophy has been presented since 1937 to honor the top team from the AHL playoffs. The trophy is made of sterling silver and mounted on a Brazilian mahogany base, with the names of recent champions inscribed on plaques.
