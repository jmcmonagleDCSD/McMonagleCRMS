American football is a sport where two 11-player teams compete to score points by advancing an oval ball into the opponent's end zone or kicking it through goalposts,

The offense uses a system of four "downs" to gain at least 10 yards; failure to do so results in turning the ball over to the defense. Key elements include the quarterback's passes, the ball carrier's runs, tackles to stop opponents, and protective equipment like helmets and pads.

Teams score points by running or passing the ball into the opponent's end zone for a touchdown (6 points) or by kicking the ball through the goalposts for a field goal (3 points). After a touchdown, a team can also attempt a 1-point extra point kick or a 2-point conversion play.
