History

Directed by Ridley Scott in 1979 the firts movie of this beloved franchise came to theatres all across the world. In May 1979 Alien made $3,527,881 on opening day ($15,985,297.55 in todays money.) After the movies success it led to 4 more movies plus 2 prequals plus a standalone story, A tv show, and finally tons of video games and comics. The Leading Antogonist (the xenomorph) caught the eye of many viewers due to its design, life cycle, and how it behaves and acts. This website will guide you through the long and complexe timeline and history of the xenomorph.

The Perfect Organism

The Xenomorph is highly recegnisable due to is biomechanical features that include an enlongated skull, eyeless skull with a retractible inner jaw (like an eal) A dark segmented exoskeleton, a long, blade-tipped tail, and claws. The xenomorph stands 7-8 feet tall 300-400 pounds, slick and agile. The xenomorph has the ability to adjust their metabolism to conserve energy.(ex: Slows heart rate down in cold environments.) They also have adaptive skin, acid for blood, and has no eyes only using body signatures and sound to see making it extremely hard to hide from this creature.
