Blacktip Reef Sharks


Where do they live and why do they live there?

Blacktip reef sharks are tropical sharks that live in warm waters. They live in a varity of places. Some of the places they live are coral reefs and mangrove forests.


  • Why do they live in mangrove forests?

One of the habitiats Blacktip Reef Sharks live in are mangrove forests. Mangrove forests are forests of plants called mangroves, which are trees with roots that are in the water, as shown below.

(Photo by Unknown from WordPress)

This is a mangrove forest

One of the reasons blacktips live in mangrove forests is because its a good place for them to grow up. As juviniles, they use the roots to trap prey, ambush prey, and more. The roots also give them safety from predators. Young blacktips also stay here because its a good place to forage for food.


  • Why do they live in coral reefs?

Another habitat blacktip reef sharks live in are coral reefs. Coral reefs are warm waters with LOTS of coral, some big, some small.

(Photo by The Austrialian Institute of Marine Science from The Austrialian Institute of Marine Science)

This is a coral reef

The main reason that blacktip reef sharks live in coral reefs is the supply of food. Since the coral makes good shelter for the fish, many fish live there, having lots of food for blacktips.


Why do they have black tips?

One of the most commonly asked questions about blacktip reef sharks is, "Why do they have black tips?"

(Photo by Unknown from Animalia.Bio)

This is a blacktip reef shark

The black tips on their fins help them camouflage from almost any angle. Their black tips help them blend in to the dark seafloor, while their bright belly blends in to the bright surface above.

Fun Facts

  • Blacktip reef sharks can swim up to 20 miles per hour

  • Blacktip reef sharks hunt in packs

  • They are one of the only sharks that can completely leap out of the water
