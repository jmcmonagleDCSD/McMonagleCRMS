Atlantic Puffins


What do they eat?

Puffins are seabirds that eat small schooling birds like herring. You may think "If puffins are birds, how do they catch schooling fish in the water?". It turns out that atlantic puffins are excellent swimmers, and flyers! Like penguins, they dive underwater to catch prey.

(Photo by Innotata from Wikimedia Commons)


How do they interact with their family?

Funny enough, atlantic puffins treat their families a lot like us! When puffins breed, they stay with their partner for life. Sometimes, puffins even have "weddings"!

(Photo by Naff14 from Wikimedia Commons)

Atlantic puffins also show that they care about their family by taking care of eachother. The male puffin will guard their nest while the female puffin searches for food. Both parents are always protective and caring for their young.


Where do they live?

Atlantic puffins live in the north atlantic ocean on grassy/rocky shores. Lots of birds build nests, incuding the atlantic puffin! While they do build nests, their nests are different.

Instead of building a normal stick nest, they build burrows! They add soft material inside the nest for their eggs, but if there is no space to dig, they will sometimes take another animal's nest.

(Photo by Charlesjsharp from Wikimedia Commons)


Fun facts

  • They sometimes use sticks to scratch their back

  • Their common nicknames are "Sea Parrot" or "Clown of the Sea"
