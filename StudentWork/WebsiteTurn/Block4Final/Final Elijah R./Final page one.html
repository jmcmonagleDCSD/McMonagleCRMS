How to play

Football is a very important sport. Thousands play or watch it every day! It first stared in 1869 in New Jersey, when the first university teams played.

In football you have to either throw the ball, catch the ball, run the ball, or even kick it.

At the start the Quarter Back (QB) throwes it to players who run a spicific rought. This happens after all the players line up at the line of srimage (Where the ball starts) and then a center snaps the ball to the QB.
