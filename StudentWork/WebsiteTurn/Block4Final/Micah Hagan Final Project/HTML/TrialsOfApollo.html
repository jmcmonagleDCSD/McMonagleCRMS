In the first Trials of Apollo book, Apollo finds himself on a dark alleyway in New York, and he is mortal! Shortly after, he attacked by two thugs, but a girl fights them and the two thugs leave. Apollo decides he needs to go to Percy's apartment, and then to Camp Half-Blood. Hwoever, he continously attacked by monsters, and the local Oracle cannot prophesy anymore. Apollo figures out that he needs to go to the original Oracle to stop Python, who is controlling the Oracle.
