Graphic Novels

Guides

As you might have guessed, the graphic novels are simply the books in graphic novel form. All of the original Percy Jackson books have a graphic novel version, and at least most of the Heroes of Olympus books have their own as well. The three Kane Chronicles and the first two Magnus Chase books have graphic novels, but the third Magnus Chase graphic novel has yet to come. The first two out of five Trials of Apollo books have graphic novels, and releases are expected sometime in the future.

The guides are simply assorted short stories about heroes and gods from the various mythologies. The Greek guides are split between the gods and heroes, but the rest of the guides are simply about the gods.
