The Heroes Of Olympus

(Or books 6-10 in Percy Jackson)

Main Page - Percy Jackson - The Heroes of Olympus - The Kane Chronicles - Magnus Chase - The Trials of Apollo - The Graphic Novels and Guides - Rick Riordan

In The Lost Hero, Jason wakes up with no memory of where he is or even who he is. He is on a field trip to the Grand Canyon when his class is attacked by a storm spirit. He is brought to Camp Half-Blood, where he learns that he is a demigod, and excels at everything he is taught.
