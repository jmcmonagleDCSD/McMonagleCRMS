Percy Jackson: The Lightning Thief tells the story of Percy Jackson, a teenager who suffers from ADHD and dyslexia. After what seems to be a hallucination at his school and an attack when he is on vacation, he learns that he is the son of one of the Greek Gods. He is then picked to go on a quest to find Zeus's lightning bolt, which has been stolen! Along the way, they discover evidence that points to Hades being the thief. They go to the Underworld to confront Hades and to get Percy's mother back, who was captured by Hades during the vacation attack. They learn that Hades didn't do it, and figure out it that it was Ares. They find Ares and get the lightning bolt back, along with the Helm of Darkness, which was Hades'. He gets his mother back and goes back to camp, where he is almost killed. However, he stays alive, and the book ends.

The next book in the series is called The Sea of Monsters. After successfully completeing his first quest, Percy comes back to camp, but learns that the tree that creates the shield that guards the camp has been poisoned! His friend Grover is also missing. A quest is declared to find something to heal the tree, but Percy isn't chosen for it. However, with Annabeth, he attempts to complete it anyway. They are trying to find the Golden Fleece, but it is hidden. They finally get to the Sea of Monsters, and find the island where the Golden Fleece is. When they try to take it, a giant cyclops named Polyphemus fights them for it becuase he is the one who guards the Fleece. Polyphemus also kidnapped Grover. In the end, they get the Fleece, resue Grover and escape, but run into Luke and a lot of monsters onboard a cruise ship. After they fight, Percy and the other get to camp and heal the tree, but they might have healed it too much...
