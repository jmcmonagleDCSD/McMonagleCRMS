In the first book of the Kane Chronicles, Carter, Sadie, and their dad are going to visit a museum, but their father tries to summon Osiris. Their father is captured by Set, an evil Egyptian god. They learn that Set is trying to create a way to summon Apophis, who will gain control of everything.
