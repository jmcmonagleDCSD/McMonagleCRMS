In the Sword of Summer, Magnus Chase is a homeless orphan who lives on the streets of Boston. When his Uncle Randolph tells him to find a sword, Magnus thinks his uncle has finally snapped. However, his uncle brings him to where he is supposed to look, and a giant who is completely covered in fire attacks him! Magnus dies, but his soul is brought up to Valhalla. He is then sent on a quest with his friends Blitzen and Hearth to find the sword and check on Fenris Wolf's bindings. If the bindings break, the Fenris will bring on Ragnarok. They also need to stop the giant from getting the sword, which he will use to cut Fenris' bonds.
