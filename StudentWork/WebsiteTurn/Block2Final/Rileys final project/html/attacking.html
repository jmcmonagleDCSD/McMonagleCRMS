Attacking/Hitting

Attacking or Hitting, in volleyball is an offensive action where a player jumps and forcefully swings their arm to hit the ball over the net into the opponent's court, aiming to score a point.



Rules

In volleyball, a legal hit requires the ball to be clearly and cleanly batted without holding, carrying, or throwing it, allowing contact with any body part. A player cannot hit the ball twice in a row, though simultaneous contact from teammates counts as one hit. A block is not considered a hit, and the ball can be contacted off the net.





Blocking

Blocking is a defensive action where front-row players jump and reach over the net to prevent the opponent's attacked ball from crossing into their court.


Rules

In volleyball, blockers must position themselves to reach higher than the net and are only allowed to contact the ball once it's on their side or after an attack hit by the opponent, provided they don't interfere with the attacker. Players cannot touch the net, block a serve, or have back-row players complete a block. A block contact doesn't count as one of the team's three hits.
