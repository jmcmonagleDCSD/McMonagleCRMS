Serving


Serving is one of the most important actions of volley ball, because without a serve the ball will never be in play.




Rules


In volleyball, a serve starts the rally and must be made from behind the end line without stepping on or over it. The server can use an underhand or overhand technique and must hit the ball cleanly over the net into the opponent’s court without it touching any obstacles. The ball must not touch the net antennae or go out of bounds. Only one attempt is allowed per serve, and a let serve (where the ball touches the net but still lands in bounds) is legal in most rule sets. The server must wait for the referee’s whistle before serving.



Underhand Serve- The ball is held in one hand and hit from below with a fist or open handed.

Overhand Serve- The ball is tossed in to the air and hit above the shoulder with an open hand.

Float Serve- A serve with little or no spin, causing it to move unpredictably in the air.

Topspin Serve- A powerful serve with a fast downward spin that drops quickly over the net.

Jump Serve- A powerful overhand serve preformed while jumping to add speed and angle.
