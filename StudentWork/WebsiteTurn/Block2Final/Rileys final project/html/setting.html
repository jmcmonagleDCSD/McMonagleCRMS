Setting


Setting in volleyball is the skill of accurately delivering the ball to an attacking player (the hitter) to enable them to execute a powerful and effective spike, acting as the team's playmaker


Rules


In volleyball, a setting rule violation occurs if a player uses their palms, contacts the ball with their hands, or if the ball rests in their hands for an extended period, resulting in a "double contact" or "lift" fault. The basic rules for setting involve contacting the ball with the fingertips, not the palms, and pushing it upward with a quick, flicking motion for a fast, versatile offense in indoor volleyball. In beach volleyball, players can contact the ball for longer, but must still be careful to avoid "lifts" or pushing the ball unevenly.
