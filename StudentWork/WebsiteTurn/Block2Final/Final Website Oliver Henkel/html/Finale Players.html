Techqniques

Also known as techs, there are many in clash royale that you might not understand

So we are going to go over them


Aggro

To draw the attention or "focus" of an enemy troop or building, making it target your unit instead of a tower.

AoE

Area of Effect. Damage or an effect that covers a specified area, such as a Fireball or Poison.

Bait

A deck strategy that forces the opponent to use a specific spell (e.g., Zap, The Log) on a low-value card, allowing a high-value threat (e.g., Goblin Barrel, Minion Horde) to pass through undefended.

Beatdown

A heavy deck archetype that focuses on building up a massive push, usually starting a high-HP Tank from behind the King Tower in Single Elixir time.

Burn

Damage that directly hits the opponent's Crown Tower, typically from spells like Rocket or Fireball, or sometimes referred to as Direct Damage.

Chip

Small, incremental damage dealt to a Crown Tower, usually by troops that only get one or two hits, or by spells.

Counter

A card, deck, or strategy that is specifically strong or designed to defeat an opposing card, deck, or strategy.

Counter Push

A transition from defense to offense, where the surviving defensive troops are reinforced to launch an attack into the enemy lane.

Cycle

1. The sequence in which your 8 cards appear. 2. A deck archetype that uses very cheap cards to quickly rotate through the cycle to play a key offensive card (like Hog Rider) repeatedly.

Elixir Trade

The difference in Elixir cost between the card(s) you play and the card(s) your opponent plays to counter your move.

Kite

A defensive maneuver to lure an enemy troop (especially a high-damage, ground-only troop) away from its intended target (the tower) by placing a cheap, defensive unit in the center of the arena, often drawing the enemy into the range of both Crown Towers.

Overcommit

Spending an excessive amount of Elixir on a push or defense, leaving you with little to defend an opponent's counter-attack.

Split Lane Pressure

Attacking both lanes simultaneously, often by playing a cheap unit in one lane while mounting a main push or a counterpush in the other. This forces the opponent to defend both lanes and divide their Elixir/cards.

Splash

A type of attack that deals damage to all troops within a small radius (e.g., Valkyrie, Wizard).

Tilt

A psychological state where a player becomes frustrated after a loss or bad play, leading to subsequent irrational or poor decision-making.

Value

An interaction or card placement that yields an Elixir advantage or significant Crown Tower damage. "Getting good value."

All of these words are used in the clash royale community a lot, so when your friend says you overcommit, you know what to do!
