Timeline of the Minnesota vikings

The Vikings were first founded in 1960,but they wouldn't play their first game till 1961. They won their first game going against the Chicago Bears. In 1969 they won the NFL Championship before the Afl-Nfl merger. In 1970 the team makes it to their first Super Bowl appearance. They do lose to the Chiefs 23-7.Super Bowl VIII: Lost to the Miami Dolphins, 24-7 Super Bowl IX: Lost to the Pittsburgh Steelers, 16-6 Super Bowl XI: Lost to the Oakland Raiders, 32-14. They have been to 4 Super Bowls and have never won. In 1982 They move from Metropolitan Stadium to the Hubert H. Humphrey Metrodome. In 2014-2015 The vikings play at a temporary location, Huntington Bank Stadium during the construction of a new stadium. In 2016 The vikings begin playing at U.S. Bank Stadium.
