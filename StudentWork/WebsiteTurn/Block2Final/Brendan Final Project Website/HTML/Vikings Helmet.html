The Vikings are most likely to be 1961 to 1979: The team's initial design featured a dark purple shell with a gold horn and a gray facemask. This classic design was used for all four of the Vikings' Super Bowl appearances. 1980 to 1984: The gray facemask was replaced with a white one. 1985 to 2001: The facemask color changed to purple, and the helmet paint became darker. 2002 to 2005: The purple on the helmet lightened slightly, and the horns were updated to the modern style. 2006 to 2012: The helmet's purple became a "fade purple," but a change to a black facemask was implemented starting in 2006. 2013 to 2018: The helmet was a matte-finish purple with a black facemask. 2019 to present: The helmet's finish was changed to an anodized flat finish, with the black facemask and horn design remaining the same. 2023 to present: A new white alternate helmet was introduced for the "Winter Warrior" alternate uniform, according to the team's official website.
