Arizona Cardinals

The Arizona Cardinals were the first ever team in the NFL and the Cardinals were one of the founding franchises, founded in 1898 in Chicago as the Morgan athletic club. They were known as the Recine Cardinals The Cardinals are also known for winning two pre- Super Bowl championships in 1925 and 1947, and for reaching Super Bowl XLIII but lost to the pittsburgh steelers. The teams location was in Chicago in 1898-1959, then ST.Louis, and Phoenix, Arizona. Their fan nickname is red sea.

Star Players

Larry Fitzgerald Jr., Larry Darnell Fitzgerald Jr. is an American former professional football player who was a wide receiver for 17 seasons with the Arizona Cardinals of the National Football League. He played college football for the Pittsburgh Panthers, earning unanimous All-American honors in 2003.

Adrian Lemar Wilson is an American former professional football player who was a safety in the National Football League. Wilson played college football for the NC State Wolfpack and was selected by the Arizona Cardinals in the third round of the 2001 NFL draft.

Anquan Kenmile Boldin is an American former professional football player who was a wide receiver for 14 seasons in the National Football League. He played college football for the Florida State Seminoles and was selected by the Arizona Cardinals in the second round of the 2003 NFL draft

Lawrence Frank Wilson was an American professional football safety who played with the St. Louis Cardinals of the National Football League
