The Minnisota Vikings

The Minnesota Vikings joined the NFL as an expansion team on January 28, 1960, and began play in the league's 1961 season, making their official debut on September 17, 1961. The first-ever game for the Minnesota Vikings franchise was a preseason exhibition match against the Dallas Cowboys on August 5, 1961, in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, which the Cowboys won 38-13. This game marked the professional debut of the Vikings and featured future Hall of Famer Fran Tarkenton.

Star Players

Randy Gene Moss is an American former professional football wide receiver who played in the National Football League for 14 seasons. Moss played with the Minnesota Vikings, Oakland Raiders, New England Patriots, Tennessee Titans, and San Francisco 49ers.

Justin Jamal Jefferson is an American professional football wide receiver for the Minnesota Vikings of the National Football League

Jonathan Raymond Greenard is an American professional football linebacker for the Minnesota Vikings of the National Football League. He played college football for the Louisville Cardinals and the Florida Gators

Francis Asbury Tarkenton, nicknamed "the Scrambler", is an American former professional football quarterback who played in the National Football League for 18 seasons, primarily with the Minnesota Vikings. He is widely regarded as the first great dual-threat quarterback in the NFL.

Graduel Christopher Darwin Carter is an American former professional football wide receiver who played in the National Football League for the Philadelphia Eagles, the Minnesota Vikings and the Miami Dolphins. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest wide receivers of all time.

Carl Lee Eller is an American former professional football player who played as a defensive end in the National Football League from 1964 through 1979. He was born in Winston-Salem, North Carolina and played college football for the Minnesota Golden Gophers. He was elected to the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2004.
