"Old cars" typically refers to classic cars, vintage cars, or antique cars, with age often defined as at least 20-40 years old, although definitions vary. Popular examples include the Ford Mustang, Chevrolet Corvette, and Volkswagen Beetle. While they offer a connection to the past, older vehicles may lack modern safety features, and purchasing them requires considering their history, mileage, and mechanical condition. /p>
