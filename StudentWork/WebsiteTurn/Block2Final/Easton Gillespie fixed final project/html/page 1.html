Quests

The quests in Blox Fruits are really important to the gameplay. The more you level up the more skill points you get to add to either your defense/health your melee/energy or you power. I will go deeper into the power on a different slide.

When you complete a quest you gain money and xp xp is used to level up and once you have enough levels you will have to travel to a new quest giver.
