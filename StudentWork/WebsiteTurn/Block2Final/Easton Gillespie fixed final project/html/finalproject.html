Blox Fruits

Blox Fruits is a skill base game were you can fight, grind, and loot. In Blox fruits there is 3 seas and multiple quests to help you level up and get to new areas or new seas. Blox Fruits was created by developers going by the name Ripindra and Mygame43 and was initially released on January 16, 2019, as a beta under the name Blox Piece but the name changed on june 9th 2019 to Blox Fruits.
