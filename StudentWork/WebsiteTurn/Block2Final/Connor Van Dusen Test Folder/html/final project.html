Fun facts about the lions

  • The Detroit lions joined the NFl in 1929. Before they were called the lions they were the portsmouth spartans
  • they are one of the four teams that have never been to the super bowl
  • they are the only team to go 0-16
  • they have won 4 NFl championships, but that was before the super bowl was a thing.
  • The lions made it to the NFC championship in the 2023-2024 season

The lions made the playoffs in 2023-2024 and 2024-2025 season.
