What are Crocodiles

Crocodiles are semiaquatic creatures they spend most of their time in places like rivers or lakes and they are found in both saltwater and freshwater enviroments. Crocodiles are cold blooded so they need a heat source to regulate their body tempature. They also have the strongest jaw in the animal kingdom.
