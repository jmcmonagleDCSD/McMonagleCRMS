Ducks

Ducks are cool and you can find them around almost any bodies of water.

Including Lakes, rivers, ponds, and small streams.

They are semi-aquatic, therfore they live on land and in water.

What They eat

Ducks are omnivores therefore they eat a variety of foods such as,

some plants, seeds, insects, fish, and fruit.

The diving duck naturally called pochards or scaups, focus mainly on diving underwater for their meals
