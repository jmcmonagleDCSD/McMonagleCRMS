The Void and the Fall of Amon

We've arrived and we play as the Protoss first. You remember Narud that battled Kerrigan. He is actually a big bad stupid Xel Naga that serves Amon. But somehow Stukov is just able to kill him by using a corrosive bolt at the end. Spam Carriers and Carriers only and you'll be all set. Make sure to take the base as fast as possible in order to boost your economy to the maximum. When the Void Thrasher appears go help Kerrigan or Raynor because this dude is tough.

Defend Kerrigan

So uhh the dude that was talking to Artanis was acually a Xel' Naga that didn't want to be slaughtered by Amon. Now you have to defend Kerrigan. You know how you do that? Siege Tanks Planetary Fortresses and literally everything else. When things get rough spam Kerrigan's little super beam.
