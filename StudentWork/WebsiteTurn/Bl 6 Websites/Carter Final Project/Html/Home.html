Chess - A beginner's guide

About

Chess is a turn-based 2-player strategy game that countries from all over the world know and play. It is so popular that it is even played at the professional level. It originated in 6th century India where the game was created to simulate warfare. It spread to Persia and then to Europe developing it into the game it is today. The goal of the game is to checkmate your opponent's king. This is generally accomplished by certain strategies and taking the opponent's pieces.The game progresses with the players taking turns moving their pieces and capturing their opponent's until a game-ending condition is reached, like a checkmate. There are 6 different types of pieces that all move differently, which make almost every game unique.

Fun Facts

  • There are more unique games of chess(~10^120) than there are atoms in the observable universe (~10^79) with possible chess games having 41 more zeros!
  • The term "rookie" comes from the chess piece, the Rook, because Rooks are generally the last pieces to be moved into action.
  • The longest chess game theoretically possible is 5,949 moves.
  • It is said that playing chess can boost your intelligence and is recommended to fight Alzheimer’s disease.
  • About 600,000,000 people know how to play chess from around the globe. Thats about 13.33% percent of the population!
