Facts About Cyndaquil



Cyndaquil is a fire type Pokemon and can shoot flames out of its back. The Pokedex states "Cyndaquil protects itself by flaring up the flames on its back. The flames are vigorous if the Pokemon is angry. However, if it is fired, the flames splutter fitfully with incomplete combustion."

Cyndaquil's number in the Pokedex is #155, which means it was the 155th Pokemon created. Cyndaquil lives in Johto, but can be found in other regions like Paldea (with the Hidden Treasures of Area Zero DLC and Polar Biome expansion)

Cyndaquil is known for its timid nature and its flames can be used to immitate foes. Cyndaquils height is usually 0.5 m, but it can be smaller or bigger than that height, depending on which cyndaquil it is.
