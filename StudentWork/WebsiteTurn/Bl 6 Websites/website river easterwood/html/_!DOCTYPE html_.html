For the highest chance of finding shiny Quilava to evolve into Hisuian Typhlosion you can you can look in Space-Time Distorsions with the shiny charm, which is obtainable by getting every pokemon to research level 10 in Legends: Arceus. To get its regular form, you can look in a certain cave in Blueberry Accedemy's polar biome with shiny charm and fire sparkling power lvl. 3.
