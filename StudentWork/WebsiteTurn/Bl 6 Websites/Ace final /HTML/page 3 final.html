Creative

what is Creative

Creative mode in Minecraft is a sandbox survival-free game mode focused on unlimited building providing infinite resources and instant block destruction and flight. It removes health and hunger constraints making it ideal for large-scale projects and creative design. Players have access to every item, block, and spawn egg in the inventory menu.
