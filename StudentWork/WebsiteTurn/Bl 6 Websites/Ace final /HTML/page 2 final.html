What Is Survival

Survival is the core gameplay experience where you are dropped into a procedurally generated world with nothing but your bare hands and a need to stay alive. Unlike Creative mode you must manually gather every block manage your physical needs and contend with the environment.

Fun Facts

  • A full day/night cycle takes exactly 20 minutes in real time
  • You can survive a high fall by landing on a bed
  • Sleeping in a bed resets your spawn point but dangerous surroundings can make respawning risky
  • wooden slabs and fences are fireproof but depending on the version/edition
  • You can use a fishing rod to pull mobs or items toward you
  • Placing a torch under sand or gravel as it falls will break the blocks into items
  • Buckets of water are essential for instantly creating obsidian or safe pathways down ravines
  • Armor stands can wear armor and hold items, including mob heads
  • You can use a composter to turn unwanted food into bonemeal for farming
  • Creepers are terrified of Ocelots and cats fleeing from them
  • Endermen will not teleport away if they are standing in a boat
  • Zombies can pick up items and armor, making them stronger
  • Villagers can wear armor and helmets including mob heads dispensed onto them
  • If a creeper is struck by lightning it becomes a Charged Creeper causing massive explosions
  • Skeletons and Zombies can wear pumpkins on their heads to prevent burning in daylight
  • Feeding a wolf bones will tame it and feeding it meat will heal it
  • You can shear a Mooshroom to get a red mushroom and turn it into a normal cow
  • Dolphins will lead you to shipwrecks or ocean ruins if you feed it fish
  • snow goloms create snow paths whereever they walk which can be farmed
  • Polar bears are neutral but will become hostile if you approach their baby
  • Parrots can imitate the sounds of hostile mobs warning you of nearby danger
  • The Warden is immune to lava making it impossible to kill with fire
  • piglins are distracted by gold itens makeing it easy to bypass them
  • Baby zombies are faster and harder to hit than adult zombies
  • You cannot sleep in the Nether or the End or the bed will explode
  • Using a bucket of water on lava creates obsidian using it on flowing lava creates cobblestone
  • Netherite gear will not burn in lava and floats on top
  • Ghasts are unable to see you through glass
  • Soul sand slows your movement speed but is useful for making bubble elevators
  • You can use gold armor to prevent Piglins from attacking you in the Nether
  • Fishing in the Nether can yield treasure, even inside a tiny hole of water
  • Fire resistance potions make you nearly invincible to Nether threats
  • Strider mobs can be saddled and used to walk across lava lakes
  • The Ender Dragon's breath can be collected in empty bottles to create lingering potions
