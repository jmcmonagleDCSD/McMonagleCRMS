Types of hackers

Black hat hackers

A black hat hacker is someone who uses their technical skills to break into computer systems, networks, or devices for malicious or illegal purposes. They typically exploit security vulnerabilities to steal data, disrupt services, install harmful software, or gain unauthorized control over digital environments. Unlike ethical hackers, who work to strengthen cybersecurity, black‑hat hackers operate in secrecy and often for personal gain—whether financial, political, or simply for the challenge of causing damage. Their actions can range from data breaches and identity theft to creating malware or running large scale cyberattacks, making them a major threat in the digital world.

White hat Hackers

White hat hackers are cybersecurity professionals who use their technical skills to protect systems rather than exploit them. They search for vulnerabilities in networks, software, and devices, but they do so with permission and for the purpose of strengthening security. By running penetration tests, analyzing threats, and helping organizations fix weaknesses before malicious hackers can exploit them, white‑hat hackers play a crucial role in keeping digital environments safe. Their work is ethical, legal, and essential in a world where cyberattacks are constantly evolving.
