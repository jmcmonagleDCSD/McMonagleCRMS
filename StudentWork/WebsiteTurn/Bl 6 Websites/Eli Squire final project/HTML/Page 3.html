Colorado Avalanche


The Beginning

The Colorado Avalanche were founded in 1995 in Denver, Colorado. The same season they won an NHL championship.

Stanley Cups

The Colorado Avalanche have a perfect Stanley Cup record, winning three in their history as an NHL franchise. In 1996, the Avalanche's first year as a franchise they beat the Florida Panthers in a 4-0 series sweep. In 2001 they beat the New Jersey Devils in a 4-3 series. Finally, in 2022 they beat the Tampa Bay Lightning in a 4-2 series.
