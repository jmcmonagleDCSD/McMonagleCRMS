The Stanley Cup

What is the Stanley Cup?

The Stanley Cup is a great reward in hockey. At the end of every season the NHL looks at all the team wins and losses and only 16 out of the 32 teams go into the Stanley Cup playoffs. In the Stanley Cup playoffs teams face off against each other. The first team to get four wins moves on to the next round. For example, the Colorado Avalanche play against the Utah Mammoths and the first game the Utah Mammoths win. So the score will be 0 to 1 in wins. First one to four wins and goes on to the next round. Now the eight teams face off against each other and so on. When there are two teams left it's called the Stanley Cup Finals and whatever team wins gets the Stanley Cup.
