All About Foxes


DISCLAIMER!

This website is meant to be viewed at half screen. Use half screen for best experience.

Foxes are a member of the class Mammalia, and they are a member of the Canidae family. Foxes are different from other canines because they are solitary, whilst dogs, wolves, and coyotes are all pack animals. Also, unlike other canines, they are true omnivores, whilst other canines are carnivorous.

There are 12 species of fox, with those being:

  • Arctic Fox
  • Bengal Fox
  • Blanford's Fox
  • Cape Fox
  • Corsac Fox
  • Darwin's Fox
  • Fennec Fox
  • Kit Fox
  • Pale Fox
  • Red Fox
  • Ruppell's Fox
  • Swift Fox
