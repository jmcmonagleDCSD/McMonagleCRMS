Red Fox


The red fox has the largest range of any canid on the planet. It lives on 5 continents and over 100 countries, ranging from Spain, to Russia, to Canada and the US, Egypt, India, Japan, and Australia.

Because of their widespread distribution, their diet is extremely large. They eat: squirrels, rabbits, hamsters, raccoons, guinea pigs, gophers, opossums, just to name a few.

The red fox has a lot of unique color morphs, that make it hard to tell if it's a different species or not. They can be red (standard), leusistic (all white), silver (silver/dark gray color), just to name a few. They have 83 unique morphs that appear just in the wild, not including ones from selective breeding, along with one extinct morph.
