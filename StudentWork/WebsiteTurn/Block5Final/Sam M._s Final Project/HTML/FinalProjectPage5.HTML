The Legend of Tikki Man
The Harmonica of the Ocean

This is the amazing sequel of Tikki Man The Mysterious Mask. This where Tikki Man is now stuck in a huge water themed galaxy which is next to the Cavepeople Galaxy but, there is 20 planets you have to go through to get to the vortex home. These planets have sea creatures, villagers, and water themed monsters. You'll have spots where you need the Harmonica of the Ocean and transform into a Bajau, one of the native people who live on these planets, and go underwater. Be prepared for that game to come out and have fun playing it.

Breeze of the Untamed

The sequel to the sequel. Tikki man now finds himself in the Untamed Galaxy where nothing is civilized. You'll have to go through 25 planets to get home so be prepared to find yourself in a series untamed planets filled with jungles, deserts, oceans, and a whole lot of monsters. Get ready for adventure, action, and destruction as this galaxy pulls itself apart. These monster are even worse then any other monster you've faced. So as Scar said from The Lion King, "Be prepared!"
