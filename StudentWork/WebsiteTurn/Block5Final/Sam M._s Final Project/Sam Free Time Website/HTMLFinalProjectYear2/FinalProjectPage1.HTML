The Legend of Tikki Man

The Legends of Tikki Man is a game that is about a man wearing a Tikki Mask that covers the man's face. The mask is called The Mask of Bristall Croyd, a crazy caveman scientist. Bristall Croyd made the mask to make himself immortal, but when he put it on he realized he'd need to gather strange orbs that have been around for thousands of centries and are all over univereses called Tikki Fragments so he put it on the shelf never to be used again. But when the scientist died his inventions were put on sale. A man bought the mask and put it on and became the Tikki Man. But the scientist had added something to the mask that he didn't test but it had worked on the man and sent him to a prison in the Autumn Galaxy and now he must travel through vortexes to find is way back to the Cavepeople Galaxy.

Levels

There are many planets where you have to travel through to get to vortexes. The 1st planet you go on is a small one because it is a prison planet in the Autumn Galaxy. It is a very easy prison to escape from because no one goes there. There are many different planets based on their galaxies. Like the Snow planets in the Winter Galaxy that are cold and have frozen hearted enemies. The Volcano planets in the Heat Galaxy have enemies that can get really heated etc. These planets have all sorts of secrets an possibly have some Tikki Fragments. You just might have to look around to find them. These planets are danerous but they're the only way back to the Cavepeople Galaxy. So you must face the dangers of the Galaxies see unique environments and face evil enemies controled by an evil and crazy leader.
