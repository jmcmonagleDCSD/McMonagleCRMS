The Universes' Creatures

The Autumn Galaxy's Creatures

The Autumn's Galaxy's creatures are different based on what color of autumn the planet is. The 1st planet is orange so there are pumpkin monsters. The 2nd planet is red so there are flying leave monsters. The 3rd is yellow so there are walking trees with yellow leaves. The 4th planet is brown so there are brown squrriels creatures. The boss is all of these things.

The Winter Galaxy's Creatures

The Winter Galaxy's Creatures are all the same inside. They all have a frozen heart and they are ruthless and bloodthirsty. These ice cold creature are on all 5 small planets. They may not have a lot strength or size but they make for in numbers. So get ready for a lot of monsters at once so have a lot brain power so you can be ready for these monsters. The boss is a huge snow golem.
