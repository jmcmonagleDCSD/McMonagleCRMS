The broncos have a top defence

The Denver Broncos defence is a top defence in the entire league. With people like Patrick Surtain, who was the defnsive player of the year. Across from Patrick Surtain, the corners are Riley Moss who was a really good corner last year, and has been amazing so far this year. And our third corner is a rookie Jahdae Barron who doesn't get much snaps, but when he is in he has made play's like getting a pass deflection or a good tackle. Then at linebacker the broncos have Alex Singleton, Justin Strnad, and Dre Greenlaw who is currently on the IR (Injured Reserve). Alex Singleton tore his ACL in week 3 of the regurlar season putting him on the IR. Justin Strnad was great last year getting 73 total tackles, 3.0 sacks, and three passes defended. At outside linebacker we have Jonathon Cooper, Nik Bonitto, and Jonah Elliss. Jonathon Copper last year was really good for the broncos getting 10.5 sacks, 58 tackles, 20 QB hit's, and a forced fumble. Nik Bonitto last year got 13.5 sacks, 48 total tackles, 16 tackles for loss, 24 QB hit, 2 defensive scores (Pick 6's). Jonah Elliss last year was a rookie and got 5 sacks, 38 tackles, 7 tackles for loss, 6 QB hits, and 2 passes defensed. At safety we have Brandon Jones, Talanoa Hufanga, and P.J. Locke. Brandon Jones last year got 115 tackles, 3 interceptions, 10 pass deflection, 1 forced fumble, and a fumble recovery. Talanoa Hufanga last year was a 49er, who got injured and put on the IR, but before he still played 7 games, and got 38 tackle. Last we have P.J. Locke who last year got 73 tackles, 1 sack, 2 pass deflections, and 1 forced fumble.Finally he have D-line who combined for a total of 63 sacks, leading the league. Broncos d-line includes Zach Allen, John Franklin-Myers, and Eyioma Uwazurike
