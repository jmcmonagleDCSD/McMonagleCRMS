Denver Nuggets


The Denver Nuggets Are A Proffessional BasketBall Team In The NBA Based In Denver, Colorado The Franchise Was Founded In 1967 As A Charter Member Of The American Basketball Association (ABA) Under The Name "Rockets". They Rebranded To "Nuggets" Before Joining The NBA In 1976

Championships

The Nuggets Won Their First Championship In 2023 Defeating The Miami Heat In The Finals They Were Last Of The Four Former ABA Teams To Reach The NBA Finals

Key Players

Nikola Jokic Was Named The First Finals MVP In 2023 He Also Won The NBA Most Valuable Player Award In 2021,2022 And 2024 Becoming The Only Nuggets player To Win The Award Doing So Three Times. In The 2024-25 Season Jokic Was A Finalist For The MVP Award. Other Notable Players In Nuggets History Are Alex English Carmelo Anthony And Dikembe Mutombo. Alex English is The Franchise's All-Time Leading Scorer
