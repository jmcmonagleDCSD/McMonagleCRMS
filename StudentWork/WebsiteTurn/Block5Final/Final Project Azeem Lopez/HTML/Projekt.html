Denver Broncos

The Denver Broncos Are An NFL Team In The AFC As of this season their record is 3 Wins and 2 losses. Founded in 1960 They have won 3 superbowls One in 1998 1999 And 2016.

Key Players

Some of the Denver Broncos Key Players over the years are,John Elway He led them to their first two superbowl Wins in 1998 and 1999. Another Key Player Is Terrell Davis He is a Hall Of Fame Running Back During Their First Two Superbowl Wins Finally The Third And Last Key Player For The Broncos Is Peyton Manning,Peyton led them to their third superbowl win in 2016

Fan Traditions

Fan Traditions are a key move in any sport it shows the fans interest in the game a tradition for the broncos is the "Mile High Salute" First used by Terrell Davis Who would salute the crowd after he scored turned into a tradition all broncos players salute the crowd after scoring sometimes. Another Tradition Is The term "Broncos Country" Its a small but supportive gesture fans or players use to show their supports for the team.
