Types

There are more than 200 species of chameleons. The most known speices of chamelons are the veiled chamelon, the panther chameleon, and the jacksons chamelon. my favorite type of chamelon is all chamelons. I can't chose, all chamelons are so cute and special in their own way. The smallest type of chamelon is the Brookesia nana or the nanochameleon. They grow up to 13.5 mm (0.53 inches long) in body length. The biggest type of chamelon is the parson's chamelon. They grow up to 60-70 centimeters (24-27 inches) in length.
