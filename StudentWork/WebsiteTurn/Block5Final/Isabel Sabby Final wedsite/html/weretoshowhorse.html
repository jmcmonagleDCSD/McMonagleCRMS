All about the Amerian Paint Horse Association(APHA)

APHA is a world wide place where you can compete you Paint horse in a lot of different events! Here is what some of the events are:

    This is were you show your horse, but you video it. Then, when you are done, you would submite it to a judge.

      Halter Million

    The Halter Million is where is one of the biggest world gatherings for halter exibits. If you win, you get $1 millon dollars. You can enter any bred into this competition.

      National Shows

    The National Shows is APHA's Western National Championship. It is regionally-focused exhibitors vie for national championships titles, prizes, and more to do with their paints.

      World Show

    It is the world's premier Paint Horse competition, it takes place in Fort Worth, Texas. About $1.5 million dollars in awards are paid out every year. In this event, it also has other things to do such as dog races, Youth Team Tournament, and exhibitor parties.

      Barrel Racing

    It is sometimes seen in rodeos. It is where you and your horse (often Paint Horses) run around barrels, they try to make it around the barrels as fast as they can.

      Horse Racing

    The Paint Horses have a very rich background in racing. It has been part of the breds identifation every since the beginning. Every single year, Paint Horses for millions of dollars all aross North America.
