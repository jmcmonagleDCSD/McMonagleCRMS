Step four

your gonna need four items for this step so be prepared to get milk, butter, cheese, and a stirring utensil

Now your gonna want to put some butter in the pot with pasta and stir around until the butter is gone, next your gonna want to put the cheese in and milk together so that the cheese will melt on the mac and cheese and mix well with the pasta, then your gonna want to stir the mac and cheese until it's consistent and good.
