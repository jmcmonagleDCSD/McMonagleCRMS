Miscellaneous

Repeters

Repeaters can extend a 15 block signal strength. Additionally, it can be ticked so it delays the output. The longer the tick, the longer the signal goes for as well.

Compariters

Yeah, comparators are complicated, but I will tell you some of the basics. Comparators detect specifics of a data block and turns it into a signal strength. For example, if you put a item in a chest, that the comparitor will have one signal strength. One item is one signal strength, 22 items for signal strength 2, 45 items for signal strength 3. For more information, vist quora

hoppers

Hopper collect items. By default, it collect from the top and sends the items down. If you turn the hopper, it will send the items another direction. Hopper minecarts will still collect the item even if there is a block on top if it.

Observers

Observers are very similar to comparators but they only detect what is happening on the face side of it. For example, if a block gets broken and changed, the observer will send a signal with a small delay to the other side opposite of the face.

Soft and hard blocks and signals

Soft and Hard blocks are things like glass as a soft block, wood as a hard one. Redstone signals can go through hard blocks and power things other than redstone. But to power redstone, it has to be direct signal from a power source or a repeter going into the block. If it is not a direct signal, it can still power things like pistons on the other side.
