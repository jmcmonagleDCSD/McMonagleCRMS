What redstone does

Redstone can do many things, but only some things. The main thing is does is powering things. Below are the things that redstone powers...

  • pistons
  • sticky pistons
  • droppers
  • despensers
  • powered rails
  • changing directions of rails
  • doors
  • redstone lamps

Each element explained

Pistons and sticky pistons

Pistons extend when powered by redstone and retract when not. They can also move blocks. Sticky pistons take the blocks back when they retract.

Droppers and despensers

Droppers and dispensers both drop items if powered, but droppers and only drop items. Though dispensers can place things if they are placedeble, like water.

powered rails and turning rails

If you are moving on a minecart and you go on a powered rail that is powered, it will speed you up. A rail that is turning will turn the other way of powered by redstone.

doors and redstone lamps

If a door of any kind is powered by redstone, than it will open. A redstone lamp will light up if powered by redstone.
