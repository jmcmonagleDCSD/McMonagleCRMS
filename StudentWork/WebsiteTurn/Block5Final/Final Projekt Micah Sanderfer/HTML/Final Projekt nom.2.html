All About Naruto

Main Storyline

Naruto is an anime about a young boy who has a demon, nine-tailed fox living inside of him, giving him power. But the thing is, this demon fox is evil!! Naruto's parents died trying to defeat this fox. They then sealed one half of the fox in the dad, Minato Namikaze, and the other half inside of Naruto. He gets bullied for having no parents and having a demonn living inside of him. He learns how to use chakra ( a source of energy allowing ninjas to use powers) and he makes lots of friends and enimies along the way as he gets stronger.
