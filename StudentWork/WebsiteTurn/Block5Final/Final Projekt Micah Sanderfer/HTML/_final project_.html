All about Jujutsu Kaisen

The Main Storyline

Jujutsu Kaisen is a supernatrual and horror type of anime that revolves around a teenage boy named Yuji Itadori that has an immense amount of physical strength eats a cursed finger that is a deadly poison buit it ends up making him become a vessel for the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna. He is then sentenced to death since he is too dangerous to be around, but his teacher, Satorou Gojo, delays his sentence until he eats all 20 of Sukuna's fingers so Sukuna can forever die. Cursed energy in Jujutsu Kaisen is a negative, destructive energy generated by the negative emotions of living beings. It serves as the foundation for the world's power system, allowing Jujutsu sorcerers to perform cursed techniques and fight cursed spirits. By accumulating enough negative emotions, humans create cursed spirits, which are evil beings that haunt the living. Sorcerers can harness cursed energy to attack, defend, and even imbue weapons, and a highly advanced application of it, known as reverse cursed energy, can heal human bodies.
