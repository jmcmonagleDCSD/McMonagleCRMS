Fish and the facts with food

Fish can eat foods like plankton, algae, aquatic plants, insects, crustaceans, worms, mollusks, and other fish. Some facts about them is that they have the ability to sense vibrations, taste with buds on their heads and tails, their cold-blooded nature, and their importance as a source of omega-3s for human health. They have lots of cool facts about them.
