Penne

Penne is a type of extruded pasta shaped like a cylinder with diagonally cut ends. The name is derived from the Italian word for "pen" or "quill," which the shape is said to resemble. Penne is popular for its versatility in holding various sauces, with versions including ridged ("rigate") or smooth ("lisce") surfaces.

origin

Penne pasta has a precise origin date of 1865, when Italian pasta maker Giovanni Battista Capurro patented a new cutting machine. Previously, tubular pasta had to be cut by hand with scissors, a method that produced irregular and crushed edges. The new machine could cut the pasta diagonally and uniformly, with the angle meant to resemble the nib of a fountain pen. The pasta was named "penne" after the Italian word for quill or pen. The invention, made in the Liguria region, allowed for consistent, high-quality production that quickly spread beyond Genoa.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb penne pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed or pureed tomatos
  • 1 tbsp tamato paste
  • pinch of red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • fresh basil
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté aromatics: While the pasta is cooking, heat the olive oil in a large skillet or pan over medium heat. Add the onion (if using) and sauté until soft, about 5–6 minutes. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  2. Make the sauce: Add the crushed tomatoes and tomato paste to the skillet. Bring the sauce to a simmer and let it cook for at least 15 minutes, or up to 30 minutes for a richer flavor.
  3. Combine pasta and sauce: Once the penne is cooked to al dente, add it directly into the skillet with the sauce. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water and toss to combine. The starch in the water will help the sauce emulsify and cling to the pasta.
  4. Finish and serve: Stir in the grated Parmesan cheese and fresh basil. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed. Serve immediately with extra Parmesan and basil on top.
