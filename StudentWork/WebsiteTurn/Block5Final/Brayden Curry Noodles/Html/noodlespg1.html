Noodles are a type of food typically made from unleavened dough which is rolled flat and cut, stretched, or extruded into long strips or strings. Noodles are a staple food in many cultures and made into a variety of shapes. The most common noodles are those derived from either Chinese cuisine or Italian cuisine.



Types

  • Spagetti

    • Image by Unknown from Wikimedia Commons

    Spaghetti is a long, thin, cylindrical pasta and a staple of traditional Italian cuisine. Its name comes from the Italian word spaghetto, meaning "thin string". Spaghetti is made from semolina flour and water and is often served with a wide variety of sauces, from simple olive oil and garlic to a hearty meat ragù.



  • Macoroni

    • image from Public Domain Pictures by Unknown

    Macaroni is a dry pasta shaped like narrow, hollow tubes. While the term "macaroni" can broadly refer to various tubular pastas in Italy, in the United States it typically means elbow macaroni, the curved shape famously used in macaroni and cheese. Made with durum wheat, macaroni is a versatile pasta staple used in dishes like baked casseroles, soups, and pasta salads.



  • Penne

    • photo by unknown from PxHere

    Penne is a tube-shaped pasta with ends cut diagonally, resembling a quill pen, which is what its name means in Italian. The hollow middle and ridged surface of penne rigate are ideal for capturing and holding sauces, from creamy and hearty ones to chunky meat or vegetable-based sauces. It is a versatile pasta often used in baked casserole dishes, pasta salads, and popular dishes.
