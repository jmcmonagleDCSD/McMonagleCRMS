Macoroni

Macaroni is a tube-shaped pasta made from durum wheat, often recognized in the U.S. as elbow macaroni used for mac and cheese. The Italian word maccheroni can refer to various tubular pastas, while the English term has roots in 18th-century fashion. Today, it remains a versatile staple used in everything from salads to soups.

Tubes vs Shells

The key difference between macaroni tubes and shells is their shape, which in turn affects how they hold sauce and their texture. While both are popular for dishes like macaroni and cheese, they each have distinct characteristics.

Tubes

  • Small, narrow, and curved, forming a "C" shape or elbow.
  • The hollow interior and bend are good for trapping creamy or chunky sauces, like in classic mac and cheese.
  • Offers a more uniform and consistent texture throughout the pasta.
  • A versatile shape used in a variety of dishes including baked casseroles, creamy sauces, and pasta salads.

Shells

  • Seashell-shaped with a hollow, concave body.
  • The cup-like shape is excellent for scooping up and holding sauces, particularly thicker, chunkier, or richer sauces
  • Can provide a slightly different texture, with a softer inside and more chew on the edges.
  • Excellent for baked dishes, creamy sauces, and pasta salads. Larger shells can also be stuffed with fillings.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz elbow or shell macaroni
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheese (your choice)
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni according to package directions, aiming for al dente. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  2. Make the roux. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the flour and whisk constantly for 2–3 minutes to form a smooth paste, or roux.
  3. Create the sauce. Slowly pour the milk into the roux, whisking continuously until the mixture is smooth and begins to thicken. Bring it to a low simmer.
  4. Add the cheese. Reduce the heat to low and gradually stir in the shredded cheese until it is completely melted and the sauce is creamy. Stir in the salt and pepper.
  5. Combine and serve. Pour the cheese sauce over the drained macaroni and stir gently to combine. Serve immediately.
